## Caption

New Document News. LlamaIndex and Kaggle just released ExtractBench, which is a benchmark for getting structured data out of enterprise-type documents. It has about 5,000 pages in it, 370 documents, and it's across eight different domains and lots of different categories.

The input is a full document with a user-defined JSON schema. Then the output is all of the values that it should extract. For the extracted values, it also asks for a page number or a bounding box, so you have citations with that.

Looking through it, there are long tables, cross-page continuations, forms with checkboxes, W-2s, presentations, dense tables, electric bills, and signatures.

This seems like a really practical dataset. I think it's brand new, so I wouldn't necessarily trust the leaderboard yet, but hopefully in a week or two everyone will be testing their models against it and we can see where it shakes out.
