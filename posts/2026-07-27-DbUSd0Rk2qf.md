## Caption

A golden dataset gives you a stable place to run experiments, but the goal is not to get to a metric you can just follow.

Early datasets may have 10, 15, 30, or 40 examples. Teams run experiments, hill-climb the metrics, and stop inspecting the underlying cases.

Every metric has blind spot and can be gamed (intentionally or otherwise). After each run, inspect the examples and traces, keep annotating, and do error analysis. 

The score gives you grounding for that review but not replace it.
