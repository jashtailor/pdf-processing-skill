## Caption

OCR on signatures is difficult and critical on documents to know if they can be used (like legal or medical documents).

In this case Gemini marked all four signatures as signed. AWS Textract found 3 out of 4 signature regions, with confidence scores from 32% to 41%.

The page has two really clear signatures and two with faint marks.  Maybe from repeated scans before the final signature, or maybe an erased signature. 

When doing OCR for signatures you need to decide what to do with these situations.  Route to a human? Do we have the original document?  Should we try to get a new document signed?  Should the document be considered signed or not, and where is the line?

It's important to include non-perfect and faded signatures in your eval set and decide how to handle them.
