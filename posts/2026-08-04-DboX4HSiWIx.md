## Caption

Agent history should not become the source of truth for how you work.

A chat captures context-specific off-the-cuff thinking. Automatically extracting every workflow from it to create skills or automations turns temporary decisions and workarounds into a structure creates a lot of process overhead that you then have to deal with on projects.

Use history to get ideas and patterns. Then decide whether each one is good, general enough to reuse, or something you should change in your workflow.
