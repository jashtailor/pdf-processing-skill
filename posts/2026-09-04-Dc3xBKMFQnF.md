## Caption

New document news. LlamaIndex added a Turbo mode in beta for much lower latency. This is super important when you have something users wait on, like uploading a document to answer questions, do retrieval, or create a report.

I compared Turbo and Cost Effective mode on the same examples from my document lab. On a JPMorgan balance sheet, Turbo finished in 10.5 seconds, where Cost Effective took 41.2 seconds, about 4 times faster. On a Vanguard table, Turbo took 9 seconds instead of 30 seconds, about 3.3 times faster. Both returned identical correct values on both documents, so there was no accuracy trade-off on either of them.

Turbo is 35 credits per page, where Cost Effective is 8 credits per page. Turbo is fast, expensive, and great for regular documents. Cost Effective is slow, cheap, and great for regular documents. Agentic and Agentic Plus are slow, expensive, but very accurate on really hard documents.

Overall, Turbo seems like a great fit when you need fast extraction while users are waiting. In these tiny experiments, Turbo delivered the claimed speed without the extraction quality trade-off. It wasn't a full independent benchmark, just a few tests.
