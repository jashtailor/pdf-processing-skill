## Caption

An AI draft can get the facts right and still not be good.

Isaac Flath and I tested this on a live session. We had a model turn a blog post into a X post, then read the drafts out loud and talked through each one. 

The ones we liked had a real number in them and one less fact, turns out cutting helped more than adding.
