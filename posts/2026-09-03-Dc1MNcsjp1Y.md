In this September 2026 video, creator Isaac Flath announces Reducto's new agentic OCR model called r-1. He explains it costs about one cent per page and is roughly 20% better than Reducto's previous pipeline. He tested it on several documents that other parsers struggled with. First, a large legal agreement with a faint "DRAFT - NOT FOR EXECUTION" watermark; r-1 correctly identified and marked it as draft, while most models missed it. Second, a document with a redline strikethrough change; r-1 preserved the strike-through, unlike Docling which he tested. Third, a rotated patent flowchart (US Patent 11,335,153); many models got the connection wrong between steps 301 and 302 versus 303, and r-1 also initially failed, so he worked around it using PaddleOCR for detection and a VLM to crop and pass the image. He ran r-1 through a 22-question private evaluation on difficult documents with maps and equations; it got quite a few right and was competitive with, and sometimes better than, giving vanilla PyHarness or Surya outputs to Chandra. He concludes r-1 is strong for high-fidelity forms, tables, redlines, and watermarks, but advises careful verification for visual diagrams.
