In this September 2026 video, creator Isaac Flath demonstrates Docling's new AFP print file reader introduced in version v2.128.0. He explains that AFP is used by IBM printing systems and that Docling now includes a specialized reader to extract text directly from these files. Testing on two public sample files, he shows the first produced searchable phrases like "C++ compiler" but also joined words and garbled characters, while the second came back mostly unreadable. He attributes the issues to an encoding limitation—the reader currently assumes only one encoding (cp500)—and notes Docling gives a warning about this. He also points out the scope limitation: AFP files can contain images and barcodes, but this MVP release only extracts text, which Docling explicitly states. Despite the limits, he concludes it is a useful start for getting searchable text out of legacy print files.
